I am a young fellow that has always loved rural sport
the faires and the patterns of Erin I used to resort
the true sons of Bachus were always in my company
till I was deprived of my Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
 
The wombs turned to earth that gave birth to my brother and me
my father and mother have gone to eternity
we worked at our trade and our money we spent it quite free
which makes me lament for my Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
 
When we were children all, we did each other adore
this little green island we wandered it o'er and o'er
my brother was taken and sent o'er the dark rolling sea
and I am left lonely for Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
 
In Dublin's fair city my brother he was pressed away
On board of a warship to Spain or the wild rolling sea
where cannons roar loudly and bullets like lightning do fly
perhaps in some battle, my Dearthairin O might die
 
He was sent to the wars to fight against Bony and France
his regiment was first in the red battle ranks to advance
but when night cast her gloom over that gory and life-wasting lee
pale, bleeding and cold lay my Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
 
If heaven would aid me and send me to Spain where he be
my life I would venture to set him at liberty
like a true loyal brother I would fight for him manfully
or I'd die in the arms of my Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
 
But now I'm alone like the desolate bird of the night
the world and its beauty no longer affords me delight
the dark lonely grave is the only sad refuge for me
since I lost my heart's treasure, my Dearthairin O Mo Chroi
