Brochan Lom

Appears On : Gàidhlig Photomac Song Sharing
Language : Gàidhlig
Lyrics: English Translation:
Brochan lom, tana lom, brochan lom na sùghain (x3) Thin porridge, sparse and thin, thin and watery porridge. (x3)
Brochan lom 's e tana lom 's e brochan lom na sùghain Thin porridge, it is sparse and thin, it is thin and watery porridge.
   
Sèisd: Chorus:
Brochan tana, tana, tana, brochan lom na sùghain (x3) Sparse porridge, sparse, sparse, thin and watery porridge,(x3)
Brochan lom 's e tana lom 's e brochan lom na sùghain Thin porridge, it is sparse and thin, it is thin and watery porridge.
   
Thugaibh aran dha na gillean leis a' bhrochan sùghain (x3) Give bread to the lads with the watery porridge, (x3)
Brochan lom 's e tana lom 's e brochan lom na sùghain Thin porridge, it is sparse and thin, it is thin and watery porridge.
   
Seo an rud a gheibheamaid o nighean gobh' an dùine, (x3) This is the thing to get from the blacksmith's daughter. (x3)
