Eilean Mo Chridhe

Appears On : Gàidhlig Photomac Song Sharing
Language : Gàidhlig
Lyrics: English Translation:
Sèisd: Chorus:
'S truagh nach robh mis' ann an Eilean Mo Chrìdh' t's a pity that I'm not on 1 the island I love ,
eilean mo ghràidh far an d'àraicheadh mi, the island of my heart where I was brought up,
's truagh nach robh mis' ann an Eilean mo Chrìdh', it's a pity that I'm not on the island I love,
eilean nam fuar-bheann àrda. the island of the cold high hills
   
'S mis' a bhiodh sona nan robh mi an-dràsd It is I who would be happy if right now I were
ri taobh na cruaich-mhònadh, an cnocan a' tàmh; beside the peat stack, resting by the hillock;
's an ceò-geal bu bhoidhche a' lùbadh 's a' snàmh with the most beautiful white mist coiling and floating
's a' tuiteam mu ghuaillean Blath-Bheinn. and falling around the shoulders of Blà-ven
   
Dhùrachdainn fuireach le cuideachd mo ghràidh I would wish to live in company with my love
'sa bhothan aig Uilleam ri tuinne na tràghad; in William's cottage by the waves on the shore
gun éisd bhi òirnn tuilleadh a dh' oidhche sa là, without us having to hear any more by day and night,
ach muir agus monadh bhith làimh rinn. but sea and moor being close to us.
   
Ruithinn-sa cas-ruisg-t' air monadh is fraoch I would run barefoot on moor and heath,
nam faighinn-sa thairis air aiseag a' chaoil, if I could get over the straights ferry,
rachainn le cabhag gu clachan mo ghaoil, I'd go hurriedly to the village I love,
's an dachaidh 's an d'fhuair mi m' àrach. and the home where I got my upbringing.
   
Chì mi am Meall, agus chì mi an Sgòrr; I will see the Meall, and I will see the Sgorr
Slinnean Churaing agus Binnean an Stòrr, the Quiraing's east slope and the peaks of the Storr,
Healabhal Bheag agus Healabhal Mhòr - Little Healaval and Big Healaval
Beul nan Trì-Allt is Geàrraidh. the thre stream delta and Geary.
