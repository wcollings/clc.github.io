Sèist: Chorus:
An tèid thu leam a rìbhinn lurach, Will you come with me my darling
Falbh thu leam mo rìbhinn lurach Will you go with me my darling
Thugainn, thugainn às seo buileach, Come, come away from here
Chan fhaod sinn fuireach aig a' bhàillidh. The bailiff won't allow us to stay
   
Tha mi còrr 's trì fichead bliadhna, I am over sixty years old
'S tha mo chiabhagan air liathadh And my locks have greyed
Saoil sibh fhèin nach olc a' riaghladh, Don't you think that it is an evil ruling
Dhol gam chuir am bliadhn' gu àirigh. To send me this year to the shieling
   
Thèid ar sgapadh feadh na dùthcha We will be scattered through the land
Mar gu sgaoilear ceò on fhùdar Like gunpowder scatters
Nuair thig na fir ud gar n-ionnsaigh When that man comes to us
A' maor ùr is Iain Tàilleir The new factor and Iain Tàilleir
   
Fear aig am bidh crodh is caoraich The man who has cattle and sheep
Gheibh e àite, tha mi smaointinn. will get a place I believe
Cha bhi na h-aithntean ri faotainn There will be no word of the commandments
Bheir e caob à cuid a nàbaidh. He will take a section of his neighbour's land
   
Fear a bhios air beagan stòrais The man who has little wealth
Gheibh e àite, bi e 'n dòchas will get a place, I hope
Air cùl gàrraidh muigh sa mhòintich Behind a wall out in the moor
Bheir do lòn às air neo bàsaich. "Get your own food or die!"
